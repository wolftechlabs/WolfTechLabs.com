CUSTOM ART

Don't have a design? We can create custom artwork for your project. Some custom art is made with the help of AI tools, and we'll tell you before we start if yours will be. We'll send you a preview to approve before we make anything. We don't copy characters, logos or brand names. If you want one of those, bring your own file and confirm you have the right to use it.

Multi-Color 3D Printing
  • Encapsulated Magnetic HardwareSeamless insertion of rare-earth magnets inside solid plastic parts mid-print. Perfect for magnetic tool racks, quick-release mounting brackets, and snapping mechanical enclosures without any nozzle attraction interference.
  • Overmolded Compliant GripsDirect multi-material fabrication combining a rock-solid structural core with soft, rubberized TPU outer skins. Ideal for custom hand grips, impact-resistant bumpers, and non-slip assistive utility designs.
  • Complex Dissolvable GeometriesPrinting internal fluid channels, zero-clearance ball joints, and ultra-smooth internal screw threads utilizing dedicated soluble support modules that completely wash away in water.
  • Heavy Workshop Tooling & JigsHeavy-duty structural organizers, drill alignment guides, custom routing templates, and industrial component frames engineered out of chemical-resistant ABS and ASA.
